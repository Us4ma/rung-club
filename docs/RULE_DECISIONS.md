# Rules decision register — double-sar-v3-continuous

The October 12 continuous-table correction supersedes the earlier manual-intent and single-deal defaults. “Confirmed” means specified by the owner’s brief; regional variants remain configurable.

| Topic | Status | Implemented policy |
|---|---|---|
| Teams and seats | Confirmed | Partners opposite, fixed parity teams. Seat numbers increase clockwise: `rightOf(s)=(s+1)%4`, partner `(s+2)%4`, play proceeds `(s+3)%4`. D always occupies the caller’s right. Shared named helpers are exhaustively tested for all four seats. |
| Opening draw | Confirmed | Only the first deal of a table uses public one-card comparisons, rank A through 2. Only tied highest seats redraw. The comparison deck is returned conceptually by creating a fresh shuffled full deck, then deal 5+4+4. Caller leads Sar 1. Comparison cards are not deducted from the deal. |
| Extreme comparison ties | Existing bounded safety policy | After 64 tied rounds, redraw distinct shuffled ranks for surviving seats. No suit priority or seat nomination. Recorded as `boundedFallback`; remains a replaceable regional/safety policy. |
| Invalid seven-Rung deal | Confirmed | Any full hand with at least seven chosen trumps invalidates the entire deal; indicator counts as caller-owned. Preserve caller, D, opening selection, table ID, points, deal number and seats. No rewards. At 64 rejected attempts, reject the call safely instead of looping forever. |
| Following suit | Confirmed | Follow the originally led suit if held. Default Open Rung void-suit rule allows every remaining card, including optional trump cutting or discarding an Ace after another cut. |
| Superior Rung | Optional named preset | Default `superior: off`, `voidTrump: optional`. Explicit `opponent-only`/`always` can restrict which voluntary trumps are used; non-trump discards remain legal. Explicit `voidTrump: compulsory` enables the distinct compulsory-cut preset. Band reveal requester obligations are separate. |
| Trick winner and Senior | Confirmed | Winning strength and effective rank determine the winner; that player becomes Senior. Eligibility never changes the trick winner or makes an Ace/trump unbeatable. |
| Automatic Bhaag qualification | Confirmed correction | No manual intent control. A Senior’s physical non-Ace, non-Rung lead is eligible. To complete collection, that same individual must win this lead and meet the consecutive-win requirement. Ace/Rung leads can win and extend a streak but cannot complete collection on Sars 5–12. There is no extra arbitrary rank threshold. Legacy intent parameters are ignored. |
| First and subsequent collection | Confirmed | Sars 1–4 stay central. First potential collection at Sar 5 requires the same person’s Sar 4 and Sar 5 wins plus an eligible fifth lead. If blocked/missed, subsequent same-person consecutive wins continue; an eligible later winning lead collects all unclaimed Sars. Teammates’ wins do not combine. Collection resets the streak. |
| Pile recipient | Confirmed | Team totals receive all collected Sars. The visible pile travels to the current caller for the calling team, or the current D seat for the opposing team, even when their partner won the trick. A public collection event records winner, team, recipient and count. |
| Sar 12 / 13 | Confirmed | Eligible collection permitted at 12. Sar 13 winner takes all remaining unclaimed Sars regardless of Ace/Rung qualification. Every deal settles all 13. |
| Ace-on-Ace | Retained exact activation | A winner whose physical winning card was an Ace immediately leads another Ace: the second Ace has effective rank 2 for this Sar, retaining suit/trump status. Physical rank never changes. Equal effective eligible ranks favor the earlier played card. No penalty for a non-leading Ace. |
| Band indicator / reveal | Retained | Only caller sees sealed indicator and private suit. Indicator is separate until returned exactly once on valid reveal. Void on originally led suit, current turn, and nonempty trick required; caller can reveal too. Requester plays live trump when available. No retroactive trump status for earlier plays. |
| Band collection before reveal | Provisional privacy policy | Automatic Bhaag qualification is disabled while sealed for every seat, so eligibility flags and collection events cannot disclose the hidden suit. An eligible lead made after reveal can complete collection; Sar 13 final allocation always applies. Needs regional confirmation. |
| Sealed final card | Retained provisional policy | At the boundary after Sar 12, auto-reveal and return indicator to prevent a caller-leading deadlock in the final Sar. Requires regional confirmation. |
| Deal scoring | Confirmed | Winning team has 7–12 collected Sars: +0 match points. Calling team collects 13: Kot, +13. Opponents collect 13: Goon Kot, +52. Sars, deals won, and cumulative match points are separate. |
| Next rights | Confirmed | Normal calling win retains caller/D. Normal opposing win: previous caller becomes D, new caller is D’s left neighbor on the winning team. Kot: D moves to previous D’s partner; caller is new D’s left. Goon Kot: previous caller becomes D, new caller is D’s left. This convention uniquely selects one of the winning team’s two seats. |
| Continuous table | Confirmed | After a 2.4s nonblocking result beat, start next deal with a fresh deck and derived caller/D. No opening contest, Play Again confirmation, seat reset or cumulative-score reset. Tutorial/advanced teaching fixtures remain single scripted exercises. |
| Leaving | Confirmed + timed default | Explicit leave replaces exactly that seat with a labelled bot. Complete the active deal legally, then wait for the remaining human partner’s Continue With Bot/Leave decision. Both humans from a team gone or no humans remaining closes the table. Initial bot seats do not count as departed humans. |
| Reconnect | Timed default | Preserve a disconnected identity/seat for 60s. Ordinary 20s turn timers continue; automated actions always use the shared validator. Grace expiry replaces the same seat with a bot. Multiple live sockets protect the identity. |
| Interrupted fairness | Conservative scoring policy | An explicit departure, grace-expired replacement, or disconnected human timeout marks that deal ineligible for Kot/Goon Kot points and completion rewards. Continuing with replacement bots remains casual/ineligible. Completed Sars and rotation are still resolved normally; abandoned incomplete deals never settle. A reconnect before any automatic action does not invalidate the deal. |
| Persistence / idempotence | Confirmed | Table ID, deal ID, caller/D, cumulative points, deals won, summaries, collection events, command receipts, sequence numbers, reconnect state, partner choices and transition deadline persist. Deal ledger keys and summary IDs prevent duplicate credit. Successor deal IDs reject stale old-deal commands. |
| Match ending / courts | Unresolved | No automatic match-point threshold, consecutive-deal courts, or ranking system added. Table continues until leaving/closure. |

## Validation approach

- `tests/continuous-table.test.ts`: exhaustive seat/result rotations, qualifying/nonqualifying leads, collection boundaries, genuine full deals, automatic transition, departure, reconnect and fairness.
- `tests/worker-room.test.ts`: actual room handlers, persisted alarms/restoration, partner decisions, closure and idempotent D1 writes.
- Existing core, legal-move, security, account, tutorial and UI regression tests remain active. Older tests that expressly required Ace-led collection were changed to exercise the newly specified non-Ace lead; negative Ace/Rung cases assert that scores stay unclaimed.
- Tutorial’s fifth lead is now the king of diamonds (non-Rung), so its real five-Sar collection demonstrates the corrected policy.

No rules are adjudicated inside visual components. `bhaag` on a played card records authoritative qualification, not claimed private intent.
